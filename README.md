This is a curated list of useful links for novelists, fiction writers, and storytellers. It includes craft resources, outlining methods, revision help, publishing guidance, querying tools, author-business resources, communities, podcasts, and software.

**Last reviewed:** June 16, 2026  
**Note:** Publishing links, contests, agent listings, and submission guidelines can change. Always check the official page before submitting work, paying a fee, or signing a contract.

## Getting Started

- [Reedsy: How to Write a Novel](https://blog.reedsy.com/how-to-write-a-novel/) - A beginner-friendly guide to planning, drafting, and revising a novel.
- [Reedsy Learning](https://reedsy.com/learning) - Writing and publishing courses for authors.
- [Nathan Bransford: Writing Advice Database](https://nathanbransford.com/writing-advice-database) - Practical advice from a former literary agent and author.
- [Fiction University](https://blog.janicehardy.com/) - Articles on writing craft, revision, and publishing.
- [Helping Writers Become Authors](https://www.helpingwritersbecomeauthors.com/) - Story structure, character arcs, outlining, and theme.
- [Writer's Digest: Write Better Fiction](https://www.writersdigest.com/write-better-fiction) - Articles on fiction craft, publishing, and the writing life.
- [Jane Friedman](https://janefriedman.com/) - Publishing industry guidance, author business advice, and writing resources.
